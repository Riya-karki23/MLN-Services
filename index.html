About MNL

Built for Scale. Driven by Speed.

Established in 2024, MNL LOGISTIC ESTABLISHMENT began its journey with a fleet of 10 trucks and a vision to provide reliable and efficient transportation solutions across the Kingdom. Through our commitment to excellence, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency, we have rapidly expanded our fleet to 45 trucks within a short span of time.

Today, MNL LOGISTIC ESTABLISHMENT specializes in express transportation and logistics services, delivering fast, secure, and professional solutions tailored to our clients' needs. Backed by a dedicated team of experienced professionals and a growing fleet, we continue to strengthen our presence in the logistics industry while maintaining the highest standards of reliability, safety, and service quality.

Our mission is to connect businesses and communities through dependable transportation services, ensuring every shipment reaches its destination on time and with care.
